My sites

The small dial on each site shows which wind directions work: green is good, yellow is OK, grey is no. N is north.

Map

Drag or swipe the bars to move through time, or tap an hour or a day. Each hour's colour is the best rating at any of your sites. ★ pins are your sites. Small pins and dots are other sites from the guide: tap one to add it. Press and hold the map to add a site anywhere.

Wave heights are forecasts for the open sea, in feet like Surfline's. Anything outside the OK range is No. Period is the time between waves. Longer means cleaner, more powerful surf.

Daylight hours

First and last light ("civil twilight") is while the sun is less than 6° below the horizon. That's about 30–45 minutes before sunrise and after sunset, longer in summer.
